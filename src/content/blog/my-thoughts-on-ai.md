AI has helped me achieve more than I could have on my own. Most of what you see on this site would not have been possible without it. That is not something I want to hide. It is a big part of how I work now, and I think it is worth being honest about.

## What it changed for me

The gap between an idea and a working product used to be enormous. There was always another tool to learn, another error to search for, another part of the system I had never touched before.

AI shrinks that gap. It helps me get unstuck, explains things I do not understand yet, and lets me try ideas that would otherwise have stayed ideas. Projects that would have taken me months, or that I would never have started, became things I could actually finish.

## A tool, not a shortcut

I see AI as a tool, and like any tool, it makes you more productive when you know how to use it. It does not remove the need to understand what you are building. If anything, it raises the bar: you still have to decide what is right, notice when something is wrong, and own the result.

The way I use it fits how I like to learn anyway. I ask, I test, I break things, and I keep going until I understand why something works, not just that it works.

## The concerns are real

There are real concerns about AI in general, and I do not want to wave them away. Nobody knows exactly where this is going, and it is reasonable to be uneasy about that.

## A new era

I think we are living through an unprecedented era, one where uncertainty and opportunity are growing side by side. That has been true of every new era in human history. The people living through them rarely knew how things would turn out, but the changes shaped everything that came after.

I am curious, and a little cautious, to see how it unfolds from here.
