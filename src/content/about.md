I build software in Bangkok. Most of what you see on this site started as something I wanted for myself: a faster way to track spending, a reason to care about an esports match, a cosy game that reminds me of my trip to Japan and more.

## How I got here

I'm naturally curious about how things work, especially when it comes to technology and software. I enjoy turning ideas into working products, exploring unfamiliar tools, and figuring out how different pieces fit together. I find satisfaction in experimenting, making mistakes, and refining my work until everything falls into place. For me, building software is as much about learning and discovery as it is about the finished product. This has led me to the path of Software Engineering and I'm currently pursuing a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science in Assumption University, Thailand.

## What I care about

What I enjoy most about building software is taking something that seems complicated or unfamiliar and gradually making sense of it. I like breaking problems down, experimenting with different approaches, and understanding why something works the way it does. I'm particularly fond of the trial-and-error process: testing ideas, learning from mistakes, and refining things until they come together. I try not to just make things work, but to understand how and why they work.
